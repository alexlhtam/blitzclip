Tank max rolls ▼

One entry per tank: WG's tank_id, the display name, and max_roll for each shell — the biggest damage one shot can do (Blitz rolls ±25%, so 1.25 × alpha). List shells in the tank's slot order — standard, premium, HE — because the replay records which slot fired, not the shell's name. Edit a value when a balance patch changes it, add tanks in the same shape, then Apply. Your table is kept in this browser; copy the JSON to share it.

Damage is rebuilt from the replay's HP updates, so the total matches the results screen — fire shows as its own row after the shot that lit it. On a killing blow the replay records only the damage applied (the target's remaining HP), so the roll was at least the figure shown (marked ≥). Time is seconds from battle start (the moment the shell landed); the second figure is the in-game countdown at that moment (7:00 clock). % of max compares each shot with the max_roll of the shell it fired, from the table above — a shot at 100% is a true max roll. The shell comes from the replay's hit records; in the rare case it isn't recorded, the shot is scored against the tightest shell it fits and marked with ? (hover for every shell's figure). No damage rows are your hits that ricocheted, failed to penetrate, or were absorbed by tracks or modules — shown with the shell's listed alpha rather than a roll.
